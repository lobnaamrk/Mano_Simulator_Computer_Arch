💌 How this works

Write Mano assembly in the editor, hit Assemble, then walk the CPU cycle one phase at a time — or let it run itself with Run All.

  • Fetch — AR ← PC, then IR ← M[AR], PC ← PC+1
  • Decode — AR ← IR[0:11], figures out the opcode
  • Execute — click once per micro-step until the instruction finishes
Memory-referenceMeaning
AND / ADD / LDA / STAAC ops with M[AR]

Register-referenceMeaning
CLA CLE CMA CME CIR CILclear/complement/rotate AC or E

Labels end with a comma (e.g. NM1, DEC 83). Addresses in ORG and instruction operands are hex; DEC values are decimal. Add I after an operand for indirect addressing.
